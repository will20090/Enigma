WHO WE ARE

Enigma is a team of four Codebusters test writers improving the test quality of invitationals, regionals, and state tournaments across the US. Our goal is to provide the same magical experience to competitors that Science Olympiad provided to us :)

Alan Cai

Hi! I'm Alan, the original founder of Enigma. Besides writing for Codebusters, I'm also a national medalist, proud coach (b2b2b state champs), and have involved myself with many other activities within the SciOly community.

NCSSM '27

Luke Choi

Hi everyone, I'm Luke from Carmel, Indiana! As a 3x State Champion and National Competitor, I have a huge passion for physics, engineering, and inquiry events (codes will always be my absolute favorite). When I'm not studying, I love playing Wordbites!

Carmel HS '27

William Huang

Hello, I'm William, and I am from duPont Manual in KY! I am a 2x Nationals medalist and I have competed in SciOly since 6th grade. My favorite events are Codebusters and XPD, and I write and ES for both of them! Outside of school, I like coding, math, and playing piano.

duPont Manual '27

Jacob Anil

Hello, I'm Jacob from DuPont Manual, KY. I love Codebusters (nats medalist) and any physics event. Outside of SciOly I love linguistics, aerospace engineering, scrabble, reading, and running!

duPont Manual '27

THE BEGINNING

Alan Cai writes his first-ever Codebusters test for SealSO B/C with Derek Lee and David Wu.

SEP 2, 2025

EXPLOSIVE MOMENTUM

Alan meets Luke Choi while writing for the Carmel Scrimmage, and they both decide to begin writing together for ~10 different invitationals.

DEC 25, 2025

FURTHER EXPANSION

Faced with a growing workload of tests, Alan and Luke invite William Huang into the group.

JAN 4, 2026

LAST MEMBER

Soon after, William convinces Alan and Luke to have his partner Jacob Anil also join the team.

FEB 3, 2026

ENIGMA OFFICIALLY CREATED

One day, Alan joked about officially having "Enigma" be the name of the Codebusters writing. While it wasn't taken seriously at first... it eventually became the group's title.
